3-Pillar Executive Action Plan

Immediate directives for margin recovery and strategic capital reallocation

Strategic Directives
01
Targeted Southern Turnaround
  • Immediately audit and renegotiate vendor supplier contracts for Telephones & Communication in the South.
  • Enforce automated discount caps on Envelopes and Office Supplies in Southern sales territories.
02
Logistics & Heavy Goods Restructure
  • Shift Tables and Office Machines away from point-to-point Delivery Truck freight toward regional distribution hubs.
  • Apply minimum margin floors (min 8%) on high-volume items like Office Machines.
03
Capital & Inventory Reallocation
  • Reallocate marketing and inventory capital to Binders & Accessories (31.89% margin) in East & West territories.
  • Investigate the North region $0.00 sales anomaly to correct pipeline tracking errors.
